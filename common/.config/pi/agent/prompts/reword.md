Say your last message again, plainer. Same content, nothing added, nothing dropped.

- One idea per sentence. Split anything I'd have to read twice.
- Plain words: use, not utilise. Base, not substrate. Way, not vector. Move out, not evacuate.
- No metaphors, no abstract nouns (surface, primitive, paradigm, scaffolding, north star).
- Name the actor: "the compiler checks queries", not "queries are checked".
- Say the mechanism or the number, not how it feels. If a line can't be restated as a fact or an instruction, cut it.
- Cut filler: "in order to", "it is important to note that", "due to the fact that".
- Say it directly instead of "not just X, but Y".
- No em dashes, no bold labels, no emoji.

Don't apologise or explain what you changed. Just the reworded message.
